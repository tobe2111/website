In wanghong marketing (a wanghong is an influencer in China with sway over their followers), results depend less on who you book than on three things you settle before booking. Is the goal awareness or sales? Where can people who watch the stream buy? How far can the price go down? If these three are left blank, the stream may go smoothly and still leave you with nothing.

Key takeaways

  • Awareness and sales call for different people, different cost structures and different numbers to measure results.
  • A sales outlet where people can buy right away inside China, and stock, have to come first.
  • A price cut during a stream stays on record and becomes the reference for other channels. Set the floor in writing.
  • Choose by the sales and returns of past streams, not by follower count.

What do you want from wanghong marketing?

What you buy from a wanghong is not the follower count. It is the people who trust that person's recommendations. There are broadly two approaches. One introduces the product through posts and short videos to plant interest. In China this is called zhongcao, which means planting grass. The other sells directly in a live stream.

If you start by mixing the two, hoping "to get known and to sell as well", you cannot set the contract terms. A brand entering for the first time has no reviews that show up when its name is searched, so building reviews usually comes before sales streams. The categories in the table are only common tendencies, and terms differ from person to person even at the same scale. An appearance fee is money that goes out whether or not the product sells. Commission is money that goes out in proportion to sales. Specific amounts and rates vary widely by person and by timing, so get quotes from several sources and compare.

Where can people who watch the stream buy?

This is the question most often skipped. A live stream only produces sales when the buy button on screen leads to a store on a platform inside China. If you do not have your own store, your product has to be listed in a distribution partner's store.

You also have to decide how the goods enter China. Holding stock locally after formal customs clearance requires a permit or registration first for some product types. Kuajing (cross-border e-commerce) follows a different procedure. We set out the difference between the two in Exporting to China: B2B wholesale or cross-border e-commerce first? Check where the stock is as well. If orders surge right after the stream and delivery is late, they come back as refund requests. If you send too much in advance, you are left with storage costs and the cost of retrieving the unsold volume.

How far should you fix the price and contract terms?

Price floor

In sales streams, it is common to be asked for the condition that "this stream has the lowest price right now". The problem is that the price does not disappear when the stream ends. Screenshots are saved and passed around, and local wholesale buyers and your accounts in other countries take that price as their reference.

So before lowering the price itself, first add free gifts or create a bundle exclusive to the stream. Set the floor you can go down to as a number before booking, and keep it in writing.

Run the numbers in advance as well. For example, if the selling price is 100, the product cost 40, the sales commission 20, and logistics and platform costs 25, then 15 is left per unit. From that you still have to subtract the fixed appearance fee divided by the number of units sold, and the loss on returned goods. Streams that sell a lot and still lose money happen when this calculation is skipped.

What to write into the contract

  1. Data from past streams. Instead of follower count, ask for the units sold and the return rate of the last few streams.
  2. Exposure terms. Write down for how many minutes and in which slot your product is introduced in the stream.
  3. Commission on returns. Decide whether commission is paid on returned orders too.
  4. Scope of claims. If efficacy is exaggerated, the responsibility comes back to the brand. Give the wanghong in advance what may and may not be said. China is also understood to have separate standards for live commerce and online advertising, so false or misleading descriptions and faking sales or view counts can become a problem. When a post written like a review carries a purchase link to sell goods, it may be required to be labeled as advertising. The specific standards and how they apply differ by product and platform and can change, so confirm the latest position with a local partner or a professional before signing.
  5. Secondary use of footage. Decide whether you may use scenes from the stream on your product pages or in ads, and for how long.

Checklist before booking

  • Can you state the goal in one sentence? Is it building reviews, or sales?
  • Is there a sales outlet inside China for the buy button to lead to?
  • Have you completed the permits or registrations your product needs, or is the schedule set?
  • Are the price floor and the gift bundle fixed in writing?
  • Where is the stock to cover orders after the stream, and how much is there?
  • Have you calculated what is left per unit after the appearance fee, commission, logistics and returns?
  • Are there already reviews that show up when your name is searched?
