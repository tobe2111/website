There is no shortcut to the top of Naver Place, because Naver does not publish all of its ranking criteria. But if you steadily cover four basics, namely business information, keywords, Naver service integrations and genuine visitor reviews, you build the foundation to be found and chosen in search.

Key takeaways

  • Naver Place is where customers first meet your business on the map and in search.
  • Wrong opening hours, closed days or photos hurt trust before they hurt rank.
  • Integrating Naver services such as booking, TalkTalk chat and coupons lets customers act right away.
  • For reviews, the quality of genuine visit reviews matters more than volume, and manipulation is penalized.

Why does Naver Place matter?

Naver Place shows a business's location, phone number, hours, photos and reviews on Naver Map and in Naver search. Many customers compare businesses through search before deciding where to go, so your Place listing is effectively your first impression. A high position also signals "a trusted business in this neighborhood".

Why integrate Naver services?

If customers can book or ask a question right from your Place listing, they act before they leave for a competitor. Choose the services that suit your business.

  • Booking: take visit reservations and payments on mobile.
  • TalkTalk: chat with customers without them adding you as a friend or installing an app.
  • Smart Order: list your menu and take contactless orders and payments.
  • Smart Call: manage calls through a virtual phone number.
  • Coupons: use them for first-visit offers or review events.

How should you build up reviews?

The healthiest approach is to ask customers who actually visited to leave a receipt review, prompted in store. A small coupon or perk keeps reviews coming in steadily. Reply to the reviews you receive so future customers can see how you treat people.

Paying people who never visited to write reviews, or manipulating reviews in any way, breaks Naver's policies and can lead to penalties. Clinics also face rules under medical law, so please read our post on medical advertising rules as well.

What comes after the checklist?

Naver Place is not a one-time setup. Search algorithms change and new competitors arrive. Record the rank of your main keywords every week so you can tell which changes actually worked.
