The Export Voucher program is a Korean government program that gives selected small and mid-sized companies a voucher they can spend on the export support services they need, chosen from an approved list. Companies also pay a share of the cost, and the application period and terms change each year with the official announcement.

Key takeaways

  • The voucher is not cash. It is credit for buying services from registered service providers.
  • Companies pay their own share, so the program is not funded by government money alone.
  • Application periods, eligibility and cost-sharing ratios change every year, so always read the announcement.
  • The goal is not to buy services but to design them so they lead to buyer contracts.

How does the program work?

  1. Check the announcement: the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy and the Ministry of SMEs and Startups announce the programs, and applications are taken on the Export Voucher website.
  2. Apply and get evaluated: companies are assessed on criteria such as export performance and plans.
  3. Selection and agreement: selected companies sign an agreement and pay their share.
  4. Use services: choose and contract the services you need from registered providers.
  5. Settlement: once a service is completed, results are confirmed and costs are settled with the voucher.

The exact range of eligible services is set in each year's announcement, so check the list before you apply.

What should you prepare before applying?

  • Target country and product: narrow down which product you will sell first, and where.
  • Export performance and plans: put your track record and goals into numbers.
  • A list of services: deciding in advance how you will use the voucher makes your plan concrete.
  • Compare providers: even for the same service, providers differ in how they work and in their local networks.

How do you turn the voucher into real results?

If you focus only on using up the services within the voucher period, you can end up with plenty of materials and no contracts. The key is to set a goal from day one, such as "close a deal with this type of buyer in this country", and choose only the services that goal needs.
