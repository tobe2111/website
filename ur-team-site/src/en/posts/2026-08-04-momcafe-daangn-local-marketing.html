For businesses that depend on customers from a specific area, such as shops, academies and clinics, mom cafes and Daangn Market are efficient channels because the people in that neighborhood already gather there. Mom cafes carry trusted word of mouth, while Daangn Market shows your ads only in the neighborhoods you choose.

Key takeaways

  • Mom cafe members trust each other, so natural recommendations readily turn into visits.
  • Daangn Market lets you choose ad areas down to the neighborhood.
  • Promotional posts written in return for compensation must disclose it, and each cafe has its own promotion rules.
  • Test with a small budget first, then expand with the areas and copy that perform well.

Why start with local communities?

Customers of a neighborhood business ultimately live within a few kilometers. Nationwide ads also spend money on people who could never visit. In local communities, your message reaches only people who can actually come, and it carries the trust of a neighbor's recommendation.

How does mom cafe marketing work?

Mom cafes are online communities on Naver where local mothers share parenting, education and everyday tips. The first step is choosing cafes that fit your product or service. Using region, gender, topic and age group, you start the conversation in cafes where your customers already gather.

  • Posting in cafes that do not fit your business can get no response or even create backlash.
  • Each cafe has rules, such as promotion boards and restricted business types, and posts that break them can be deleted.
  • Posts written in return for money or perks must disclose it under the Korea Fair Trade Commission's guidelines on endorsements and testimonials.

How are Daangn Market ads different?

Daangn Market, also known as Karrot, is a local community and secondhand trading app with 39 million registered users as of 2024. You can set your ad range anywhere from a single neighborhood to the whole country, and there is a simple mode for first-time advertisers. There are two main ad types.

  • Native feed ads: shown naturally between posts on the home feed, good for raising awareness and building regulars.
  • Catalog product ads: shown between search results to drive sales from people with buying intent.

An efficient approach is to concentrate budget on evenings and weekends when users are most active, test with a small budget first, and then expand the ads that perform well.
