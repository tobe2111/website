If your shop or clinic serves foreign tourists or foreign residents in Korea, start by tidying up your Google Business Profile. Visitors from abroad often look for places with the Google Maps and Google Search they already use. Listing is free, and getting the English information, photos and review replies right makes a big difference to first impressions.

Key takeaways

  • Google Business Profile is a free service that shows your business information on Google Maps and Search.
  • Think of Naver Place for Korean customers and Google for foreign visitors, and keep the information identical on both.
  • An English business name, an accurate map pin, opening hours and entrance photos matter most to foreign visitors.
  • Reply to reviews in the language they were written in; it shows the next customer how you treat people.

Why do you need a Google Business Profile?

Naver Map is the default in Korea, but visitors from abroad often open the Google Maps they use at home first. Some Google Maps features, such as certain directions, are limited in Korea, but it is still widely used to look up places and check hours, reviews and photos. If your profile is empty or wrong, visitors go to another place with better information.

What should you fill in?

Create a Business Profile with a Google account and complete ownership verification using the method Google offers you. Then fill in the items below. Foreign visitors often find Korean signs hard to read, so exterior and entrance photos help a lot with finding you.

Clinics should list their specialties and whether consultations are available in other languages accurately, while avoiding wording that promises results or compares them with other clinics, just as in Korean advertising.

How do you use it alongside Naver Place?

It is not a choice between the two but a division of roles. The most common mistake is updating one and forgetting the other. When hours or phone numbers change, update both on the same day. For Naver Place, see a basic checklist for ranking higher on Naver Place.

Checklist after listing

  • Is ownership verified and the profile public?
  • Do the English name and map pin match your real entrance?
  • Are public holidays and temporary closures included?
  • Are exterior, entrance and interior photos up to date?
  • Do hours, address and phone match your Naver Place listing?
  • Are you replying to new reviews in the reviewer's language?
