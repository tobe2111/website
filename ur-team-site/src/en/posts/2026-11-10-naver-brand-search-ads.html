Naver Brand Search ads show your brand content on its own at the very top of Naver's integrated search results when someone searches your brand name. Rather than bringing in new customers, they make sure the customers already looking for your brand do not slip away, and guide them to accurate information.

Key takeaways

  • When people search keywords related to your brand, your ad appears at the top with text, images and video.
  • You can register up to 30 keywords and 5 creatives, and run campaigns for 30 to 90 days.
  • The minimum ad spend is KRW 600,000, and pricing varies with keyword search volume.
  • It works best once your brand already has a meaningful amount of search volume.

What does a Brand Search ad look like?

When someone searches your brand name, a large block with your brand's images, introduction, shortcut menus and video appears above the regular search results. Customers see your official information and latest promotions at a glance and can go straight to the page they want.

How is the cost decided?

ItemDetails
KeywordsUp to 30 keywords related to your brand
CreativesUp to 5
PeriodMinimum 30 days, maximum 90 days
PricingSet by product type, run period and total keyword search volume over that period
Minimum spendKRW 600,000

Unlike Power Link, where every click costs money, you pay for the placement over a fixed period, so you can lock in your budget in advance.

When does it work best?

  • When brand searches are growing: for example after press coverage, review campaigns or outdoor ads lead more people to search your name
  • Product launches and seasonal events: when you want searchers to see your latest news first
  • When other results rank above you: when searching your brand name shows other sellers or outdated posts before your official information

What limits should you know before starting?

Brand Search ads only reach people who already know your brand. If your brand is new and search volume is very low, the same budget may do more in ads that build awareness first. Because these searchers already came looking for you, performance metrics tend to look strong, so evaluate them separately from ads that bring in new customers.
