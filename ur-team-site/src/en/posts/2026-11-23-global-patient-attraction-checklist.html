Attracting international patients should not start with ads. The safe order is to confirm requirements such as registration as a medical institution that attracts foreign patients, set up patient support inside the clinic, and only then promote through channels in the target country.

What follows is general preparation guidance. Registration requirements and procedures can change, so check the latest guidance from the relevant authorities before you proceed.

Key takeaways

  • Korean medical institutions that want to attract foreign patients must go through a government registration process.
  • Set up interpretation, multilingual consent forms, payment and post-return care first.
  • Patients look for clinics on different platforms in each country, so choose channels country by country.
  • You need to follow both Korean medical law and the rules of local platforms.

Is registration required to attract foreign patients?

Yes. A Korean medical institution that wants to attract foreign patients must register with the Ministry of Health and Welfare as an institution that attracts foreign patients, and the Korea Health Industry Development Institute provides practical guidance. There are requirements such as specialists in each department and medical malpractice liability insurance, so start by confirming that your clinic meets them.

What should you prepare inside the clinic?

ItemWhat to check
Consultation and interpretationDo you have staff or an interpretation setup for the patient's language?
Multilingual documentsAre consent forms and pre and post treatment instructions ready in the patient's language?
Pricing informationIf pricing differs from domestic patients, are the basis and the way you explain it clear?
PaymentCan you accept overseas cards and the payment methods patients use at home?
Response hoursHave you set hours and a person in charge of inquiries, allowing for time differences?
AftercareIs there a way to follow up after patients return home, and a contact route if problems arise?

Which countries and channels should you use?

How patients find clinics varies by country. Chinese patients often look for clinic information on Xiaohongshu, a lifestyle and review app, and Douyin, the Chinese version of TikTok, so registering as an official clinic on these platforms is where trust begins.

UrTeam works with a partner in China with roughly KRW 300 billion in scale to register clinics as official hospitals on Xiaohongshu and Douyin. As of 2025, all six hospital groups we worked with were successfully registered. Alongside patient attraction, we can also promote clinic-exclusive OEM products in the local market.

Whichever country you target, remember to follow both Korean medical law and the local platform's medical advertising rules.

Pre-launch checklist

  1. We have confirmed the registration requirements and process for attracting foreign patients.
  2. We have chosen the main treatments to promote and the target countries.
  3. Interpretation, multilingual documents and payment methods are ready.
  4. We have assigned a person and hours for handling inquiries.
  5. We have checked the main platforms and advertising rules in the target countries.
  6. We have set out aftercare and a response process for problems after patients return home.
