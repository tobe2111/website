FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Q1Do you really avoid Coupang and SmartStore?

Yes. We avoid open marketplaces where prices are exposed, and sell through group buys, live commerce, closed malls and B2B supply that keep your price intact.

Q2Is there upfront cost or inventory risk?

We design a sales structure with no upfront investment risk. After agreeing on the supply price, UrTeam can also buy your products outright and distribute them.

Q3How do I see sales?

We share detailed settlement and sales data. We never hide where distribution revenue comes from.

Q4Can you handle excess or near-expiry stock?

Yes. If the supply price works, we analyze and buy it ourselves and move it quickly at home or abroad.

Q5Can we export after domestic distribution?

Yes. Sales data proven in Korea opens the door to B2B deals with overseas buyers and local B2C sales. See Global Export.

FAQ

Q1Which countries can you export to?

Seven regions: the US, India, China, Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Japan and Europe. The approach differs by country, from B2B buyers to B2C platforms; see the country services below.

Q2Can we use the Korean export voucher?

US entry and Middle East B2B matching can be linked with the export voucher program. We check eligibility in the consultation.

Q3How are fees structured?

It depends on the service. The US and India use a success-based fee model, while services such as SNS marketing are run on agency fees. We explain the structure before we start.

Q4Do you help with certification and regulation?

Yes. From regulatory and certification consulting in India to CPNP in Europe, we handle each country's requirements with local partners.

Q5We worry about one-off exports.

We aim for long-term partnerships with transparent settlement, until your brand generates sales on its own in the local market.
