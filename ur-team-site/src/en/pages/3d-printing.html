First time ordering?

Know these basics
and we can move faster

You do not have to. Send a file or sketch and we will choose for you. Knowing the following just makes it easier to describe what you want.

There are two printing methods

FDM

Melted filament, built layer by layer

Surface
Layer lines are slightly visible
Cost
Low, materials are inexpensive
Size
Good for large parts
Best for
Prototypes, enclosures, large objects, functional parts
SLA

Liquid resin, cured with light

Surface
Smooth and finely detailed
Cost
Higher than FDM
Size
Suited to small, precise parts
Best for
Figures, small precise parts, anything where surface matters

Choose the material by how it will be used

PLA

The default material. Good for form-check prototypes and display pieces. Not for hot environments.

ABS

Handles heat and impact better than PLA. Used for working enclosures and functional parts.

Resin

For small parts that need fine detail. Smooth surface. Avoid long-term sun exposure.

Carbon · glass fiber reinforced

Stiff and resistant to bending. Used for load-bearing mechanical parts.

Three rules if you model it yourself

Keep walls at 1.5 mm or more

Walls that are too thin break or warp during printing. We recommend 1.5–2 mm.

Overhangs beyond 45° need supports

Anything hanging in the air is printed on supports that are removed afterwards. They can leave marks.

Leave a gap between mating parts

Parts drawn to the exact same size will not fit. A gap of around 0.5 mm is typical.

These are common rules of thumb. They vary by machine and material, so we will tell you the right values once we see your file.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Q1Can you do complex shapes? Can you model from just an idea?

Yes. Complex shapes are a chance to show what we can do, so they are always welcome. Without a drawing, a sketch or photo is enough for us to model it.

Q2When will I receive it?

It depends on size and difficulty, usually 3 to 7 days. Complex shapes can take longer, but we stand behind the quality.

Q3Do you take large orders?

Yes. We run 41 3D printers. Ask and we will book machine time right away.

Q4Do revisions cost extra?

Modeling includes two revisions. After that there may be a charge depending on time and cost. Simple fixes are free.

Q5Can I order only modeling or only printing?

Yes. We do not currently offer post-processing such as sanding or painting.

Q6What file should I send?

We accept STL, STEP, OBJ and 3MF. If you are not sure of the format, just send what you have. The form takes one file up to 5 MB. For anything larger, use a share link, KakaoTalk or email.

Q7Do you work on weekends?

If you contact us before noon on Saturday (Korea time), we work that Saturday.

Q8Which machines do you use?

For FDM: Replicator+, MOMENT2, M350-MOMENT, Z18 and Bambu. Ask us about SLA. Build sizes are in the equipment table.
