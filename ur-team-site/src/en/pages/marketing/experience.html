UrTeam's overall reviewer program strategy

Six steps from goals to feedback

Goal setting and targeting up front

We define integrated goals such as brand awareness, traffic and conversions.

Content optimization

We meet content requirements such as SEO optimization and detailed reviews, and monitor the conversion landing page for any issues.

Reviewer selection and management

We review each reviewer's channel influence, product relevance and past collaboration results, then manage schedules and content review in one place.

Integrated campaign operation and amplification

We plan and execute strategies that make the most of the content created, such as cross-promotion and paired advertising.

Performance measurement and data analysis

We analyze reach, engagement, conversion rate and ROI, and track results with GA, Insights and ad tools.

Ongoing feedback

We monitor content throughout the program and give continuous feedback.
