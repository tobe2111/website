Placements UrTeam recommends

We use both together

Recommended placement 1

Google Search Ads

Ads appear right at the top when a user searches for a specific keyword. Because they reflect search intent, meaning the user is already interested in the product or service, they reach potential customers with high purchase intent directly and deliver high conversion rates.

Targeting is precise and billing is cost per click (CPC), so budgets are easy to manage. Reach can extend to partner sites through the Google Search Network, making these ads especially effective for driving specific actions such as product purchases or service exploration.

UrTeam strategy
We focus on search terms with high purchase intent (e.g. "best laptop 2025") to raise conversion rates.
Preventing overlap
After choosing key keywords, we set them as negative keywords in PMAX to prevent duplicate exposure.
Recommended placement 2

Google PMAX (Performance Max)

It covers all of Google's ad channels in a single campaign, including Search, Display, YouTube, Gmail, Discover and Maps. AI places ads in the right spots all at once, saving time and effort.

Google's machine learning optimizes bidding and ad placement in real time and finds on its own which combination of the text, images and video you provide works best. You only set the goal (conversions, ROAS and so on) and the rest is handled automatically, so it works well even for advertisers with limited resources.

Once you set a goal (a purchase or an inquiry), PMAX lets Google combine your ads across search, YouTube, Gmail and other placements. It works best when conversion tracking is set up correctly, and fits clear goals such as e-commerce or lead generation.

UrTeam's overall Google ad strategy

Conversions with Search Ads,
new customers with PMAX.

Running PMAX and Google Search Ads together maximizes the strengths of each. Search Ads raise conversion rates through precise targeting, while PMAX brings in new customers through broad reach. UrTeam reduces overlap through regular monitoring and optimization, and uses the latest updates to optimize campaigns.
