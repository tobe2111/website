FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Q1Is the consultation free?

Yes. With a consultation we also offer free Naver ad consulting or setup, free consulting on government support programs, and a basic brand identity review.

Q2How are ad spend and fees set?

It depends on the service. Some, like search ads, charge per click (CPC) so you pay only for what you use; others, like Naver brand search ads, are priced by period and search volume. You get a quote with every cost listed before we start, with no hidden fees.

Q3How long is the contract?

Three months, then you renew. We never push long-term contracts.

Q4How do I see results?

Regular reports share metrics such as impressions, clicks and inquiries along with the next plan. A KakaoTalk room with our CEO and team lets us act on feedback right away.

Q5I don't know which channel to start with.

That's fine. In the consultation we look at your industry and situation and recommend starting with the weakest stage on the journey map above. You don't need to hand over every channel at once.

Q6Do you work with hospitals?

Yes. Hospital marketing is run separately to meet medical advertising rules. See Hospital Marketing and Global Patients.
