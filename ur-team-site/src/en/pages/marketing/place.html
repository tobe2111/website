What is Naver Place

Over 70% of customers
decide where to go after searching.

Naver Place is a service that shows a business's location, information, reviews, photos and more on Naver Map and in Naver search.

From basic details such as location, phone number, opening hours, closing days and photos to reviews from past customers, it shows everything at a glance. It is where your first encounter with customers takes place.

The image a top Place ranking creates

Being at the top
builds trust on its own.

Expertise

A top ranking makes you look like a brand with expertise and capability in your industry. The higher you appear, the stronger the impression of "a business you can trust in this field."

Hot spot

The number one Place listing is not just a frequently viewed business. It creates the image of a "hot spot" that people are actively visiting right now, which leads to organic visits and social media buzz.

Trust

Data such as review counts, ratings and customer responses make you recognized as a trustworthy business. It eases customer doubts from the very first search and speeds up their decision.
