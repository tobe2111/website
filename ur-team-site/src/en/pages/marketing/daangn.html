UrTeam's ad strategy

We run campaigns in three parts

Precise targeting · content optimization

We target precisely by region and demographics, and refine content with eye-catching thumbnails and concise copy. For example, home appliance ads run in the evening in higher-income areas.

Peak-time focus · small-budget tests

We concentrate budget on evenings and weekends when users are most active, test with small budgets first, then scale up the best-performing ads.

Analysis · A/B testing · offers

We analyze click-through rate (CTR), CPC and conversion rate, keep improving through A/B tests, and drive purchases with offers such as coupons and discounts.

UrTeam's overall Daangn ad strategy

Build awareness with native ads,
sell with catalog ads.

Feed ads raise early brand awareness, while catalog product ads turn customers with purchase intent into conversions. That is why we first set the goal (awareness vs. conversion) and target customers for each ad.

We split the budget evenly, adjust it regularly based on click-through rate (CTR) and conversion data, and link native ads to catalog pages to drive conversions.

More Daangn ad tips
  • Use local landmarks and neighborhood features
  • Run ads mainly in the evening and on weekends when users are active
  • Test several strategies with small budgets, then increase spend gradually based on results
  • Monitor key metrics such as click-through rate (CTR) and conversion rate regularly
  • Boost conversion rates with offers such as coupons and discounts
  • Prepare a mobile-friendly landing page before launch
