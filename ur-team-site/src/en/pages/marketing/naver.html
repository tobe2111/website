Ad products

5 types of ads
we run on Naver

01 · Naver SA

Power Link

When a user searches for a specific keyword, an ad with your site name, description and URL is shown. Rank is decided by bidding on each keyword, and when maximum bids are equal, Quality Score determines the rank.

Display
Site name, description, URL
Bid
KRW 70 to KRW 100,000 per keyword
Billing
CPC (Cost Per Click), each time a user clicks the ad
Example
Rank 3 for the keyword 'study in Canada', bid KRW 100, 100 clicks = KRW 10,000 in ad spend
02 · Naver SA

Shopping Search Ads

Store products are shown as image ads in the shopping section of integrated search and on Naver Shopping search results pages. When a keyword is searched, 2 to 4 ads appear at the top of integrated search, with the number depending on the keyword and display type.

Setup
Products must be registered on Naver Shopping before ads run
Rank
Bid per product and keyword-to-product relevance
Billing
CPC, cost per click
03 · Naver SA

Content Search Ads

On keywords where people research in depth, advertisers who are experts in the field deliver accurate, trustworthy information through blog, post and cafe content. Ads appear in Naver PC and mobile integrated search, news, blogs and more.

Display
PC and mobile integrated search, news, blogs and more
Billing
CPC
04 · Naver SA

Brand Search Ads

When a user searches for a brand keyword, the brand's latest content is shown exclusively at the very top of integrated search results as text, images and video.

Keywords
30 brand-related keywords registered
Creatives
Up to 5
Period
Minimum 30 days to maximum 90 days
Billing
Price set by product type, display period and total keyword search volume for the period
Minimum ad spend
KRW 600,000
05 · Performance DA

Display ads (GFA)

With detailed targeting and real-time bidding, these ads bring in potential customers from premium placements such as the Naver mobile main page, Smart Channel, sub pages and the Band app. These placements reach Naver's 30 million daily active users.

Age, region and interest targetingNaver mobile main + Band at onceBanner, video and native imagePerformance tuned by real-time bidding

Naver GFA is the Naver display ad product whose ad cost is set by real-time bidding.
